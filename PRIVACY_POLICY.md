# Privacy Policy for Juthor Academy (جذور أكاديمي)

**Last Updated:** February 12, 2026

## Introduction

Welcome to Juthor Academy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application. Please read this privacy policy carefully. If you do not agree with the terms of this privacy policy, please do not access the application.

## Information We Collect

### Personal Information
We may collect personal information that you voluntarily provide to us when you:
- Register for an account
- Subscribe to courses
- Use the wallet feature
- Contact our support team
- Participate in quizzes and assessments

Personal information may include:
- Full name
- Email address
- Phone number
- Student ID
- Date of birth
- Profile picture
- Parent/guardian information (for minor students)

### Educational Data
We collect information related to your educational activities:
- Course enrollment and progress
- Lesson completion status
- Quiz scores and results
- Video watch history
- Study time and engagement metrics
- Certificates and achievements

### Financial Information
When using the wallet feature:
- Wallet balance
- Transaction history
- Recharge codes used
- Course purchase records
- Payment timestamps

**Important:** We do NOT store credit card numbers, bank account details, or other sensitive payment information. All financial transactions are processed through secure third-party payment processors.

### Device and Usage Information
We automatically collect certain information when you use our app:
- Device type and model
- Operating system version
- Unique device identifiers
- IP address
- App version
- Usage statistics and analytics
- Crash reports and error logs

### Permissions
Our app may request the following permissions:
- **Camera:** For profile picture upload and document scanning
- **Photo Library:** For selecting images from your device
- **Notifications:** For course updates, lesson reminders, and important announcements
- **Network Access:** For downloading course content and streaming videos

- Providing access to courses and educational content

### Account Management

### Wallet and Payments
- Processing course purchases
- Managing wallet balance and transactions
- Validating recharge codes

- Preventing fraud and unauthorized access

### App Improvement
- Analyzing usage patterns
- Improving app performance
- Fixing bugs and technical issues
- Developing new features
- Conducting research and analytics

### Communications
- Sending course updates and announcements
- Notifying you about new content
- Responding to your inquiries
- Sending promotional materials (with your consent)

We may disclose your information if required to:

## Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your information:
- Encryption of data in transit (HTTPS/TLS)
- Secure authentication mechanisms
- Regular security audits
- Access controls and authentication
- Screen recording protection (on iOS devices)
- Watermarking on video content to prevent unauthorized sharing

However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure. While we strive to protect your information, we cannot guarantee absolute security.

## Data Retention

We retain your information for as long as:
- Your account is active
- Necessary to provide our services
- Required by law or for legitimate business purposes

When you delete your account:
- Personal information will be deleted within 30 days
- Some information may be retained for legal compliance
- Anonymized data may be retained for analytics
